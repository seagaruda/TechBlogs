## SeaGaruda

I am an engineer working on industrial software and cloud infrastructure:
metering platforms, SCADA integration, energy data pipelines, and the
unglamorous work of moving them onto public cloud without breaking them.

This blog collects what actually held up in delivery — not vendor summaries.
Every article is written after the code, the migration, or the outage.

## What you will find here

- **Cloud migration** — what changes when bare-metal assumptions meet
  elastic infrastructure, especially inside database code.
- **Architecture** — when load balancing is enough and microservices are
  premature; how to size a monolith-to-distributed move honestly.
- **Databases** — managed MySQL/PostgreSQL in practice, time-series
  workloads, and the parameters nobody warns you about.
- **OT security** — the Purdue Model read as a logical data-flow contract
  rather than a floor plan.
- **Digitalization** — why systems go live and processes stay in WeChat
  groups and Excel.
